HOTDOG
HUSTLE 3D

New shift. New rules. You now walk around a 3D kitchen. Grab a bun, sausage and toppings from their stations, carry one item at a time, and place them on the tray. Ingredients snap into place to build the hotdog automatically.

How to play
1. Walk to the bun rack and press E / INTERACT.
2. Carry it to the tray and interact to place it.
3. Repeat with sausage and the exact toppings the customer requested.
4. Walk to the service bell and interact to serve the order.

Desktop: WASD to walk, mouse to look, E to grab/place, click the game to capture the mouse.
Mobile: left joystick to move, drag the right side to look, and use the on-screen INTERACT / SERVE buttons.

VIP customers are rare (about 1 in 35 arrivals) and order a big pile of food for a huge reward. You have 120 seconds. Wrong orders cost time; limited tray slots mean you must plan ahead.
